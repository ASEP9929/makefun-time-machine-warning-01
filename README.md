Entry for the make.fun bounty [Funniest Time Machine Warning Label](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-time-machine-warning-f8add151): one warning label for a time machine, at most 120 characters, judged on how funny it is.

## The entry

> WARNING: Do not meet yourself. Neither of you is insured, and both of you will insist on driving.

The hazard is real — meeting your own past self — and the joke turns it into a deadpan safety notice: two copies of you, neither insured, both insisting on the wheel. The surprise lands in the last few words, exactly where a warning label's punch should sit.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
```

Output is deterministic: 97 characters, one line, byte-identical on every run.
